What's Inside

Everything your campus needs

Six core modules built to make student life smoother, smarter, and more connected.

📣
Feed & Stories

Stay in the loop with real-time campus updates, stories, and announcements. Share moments, ideas, and opportunities with your peers.

📦
Delivery

Order food, essentials, or notes from classmates. Earn by delivering across campus — fast, peer-to-peer, and cashless.

💼
Freelancing

Post micro-gigs or find talent on campus. Design, coding, tutoring, writing — monetize your skills between classes.

🎉
Events

Discover clubs, hackathons, fests, and workshops. RSVP, get reminders, and never miss what's happening on campus.

💳
Wallet

A lightweight campus wallet for seamless payments. Pay for deliveries, gigs, and events without leaving the app.

🤝
Team Finder

Find teammates for projects, hackathons, or startups. Filter by skills, interest, and availability to build your dream squad.
